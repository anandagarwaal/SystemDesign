# Mission: System Design for L6/L7 Interviews

## Why
Pass system design rounds at the Staff/Senior-Staff (L6/L7) level at FAANG-tier
companies. The bar there is not "can you draw a working system" — it's "can you
drive a 45-minute design proactively, go deep on the hard parts, and defend
tradeoffs with the confidence of someone who has built this before."

## Success looks like
- Drive any of hellointerview's 27 case studies end-to-end using their delivery
  framework, unprompted, in ~35 minutes.
- For each design: nail Functional + Non-Functional Requirements, do capacity
  estimation when it changes the design, define a clean API, draw a high-level
  design, then go DEEP on 2-3 bottlenecks without being asked.
- Speak the language fluently: CAP, sharding, consistent hashing, quorum,
  fan-out, CDC, idempotency, back-of-envelope math — and know when each matters.
- Hit the L6/L7 signal: ~40% breadth / 60% depth, simple-by-default designs,
  proactive deep dives, clear judgement on alternatives, no over-explaining the 101.

## Constraints
- Starting as a beginner: building blocks (caches, queues, sharding, consistency)
  are still fuzzy. Need foundations before depth.
- No deadline — optimize for deep, durable mastery over speed.
- Learning preference: small, tightly-scoped lessons with retrieval practice;
  framework-first, then core concepts, then case studies easy → hard.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Coding/DSA interview prep.
- Behavioral/leadership rounds.
- Company-specific quirks beyond general FAANG-style expectations.
