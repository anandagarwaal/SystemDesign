Mock Interview Trainer

Success = driving a design end-to-end in ~35 minutes, unprompted. Start the timer, draw a random prompt, talk out loud, and hit the deep-dive triggers before the interviewer asks.

35:00 Target: ~35 min · 5 reqs · 5 entities/API · 10 HLD · 13 deep dives · 2 wrap

The delivery framework — drive this every time

  1. Functional requirements — 3 core "users can…"; cut scope out loud.
  2. Non-functional requirements — pick 3–5 -ilities; name the CAP stance + scale.
  3. Capacity estimation — only the numbers that change the design (see the cheat sheet).
  4. Core entities & API — 3–4 nouns; clean endpoints; idempotency + pagination.
  5. High-level design — boxes & arrows; trace one happy-path request.
  6. Deep dives (60%) — attack 2–3 bottlenecks proactively; name tradeoffs.

L6/L7 signal: ~40% breadth / 60% depth · simple-by-default · proactive · judgement on alternatives · don't over-explain the 101.
