Spaced Review

Start every session here. Retrieval on a forgetting-curve schedule, cumulative section exams, and a drill on whatever you keep missing.

How this works. Every question you answer in a lesson is scheduled for review: a miss comes back the next day; a first-try correct comes back after 1 day, then 3, then growing gaps. Here, only your first answer counts and options are shuffled, so you have to recall the idea, not the letter. About half the questions are intuition questions ("why", "what breaks if", "picture it"). On those you write your answer in your own words first; the options unlock only after you commit. If you only remember the steps, those are the ones you'll miss, and what you wrote goes into the report so your teacher can find the gap.
Scheduling is SM-2-lite in reference/mastery.js. Your progress lives in this browser's localStorage, so use the Report tab to hand results to your teacher in the chat. Question bank: reference/question-bank.js (rebuild with python3 tools/build_question_bank.py).
